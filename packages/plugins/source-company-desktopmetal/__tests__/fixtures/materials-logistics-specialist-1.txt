Materials & Logistics Specialist
Location: Irwin, PA
Position Summary
We are seeking a full-time, detail-oriented and dependable Materials / Logistics Specialist to
support daily shipping, receiving, inventory, and resin material preparation operations. This role
is responsible for ensuring accurate order fulfillment, proper freight packaging, timely shipment
of goods to domestic and international customers, and accurate preparation of resin materials in
support of production activities.
The ideal candidate thrives in a fast-paced environment, demonstrates strong attention to detail,
and works well within a collaborative team setting.
This position will be responsible for:
Shipping & Receiving
● Pack parcel shipments to ensure safe transit and delivery to domestic and international
customer locations
● Prepare commercial export documentation, including Commercial Invoices (CI) and
Shipper’s Letter of Instruction (SLI)
● Package and band LTL and TL freight shipments for safe transport
● Load trucks for outbound customer shipments
● Perform receiving functions, including unloading trucks and inspecting incoming
shipments for accuracy (parts and quantities)
● Verify shipments against Purchase Orders using the ERP system
● Maintain inventory of shipping supplies and place orders as needed
● Monitor and respond to emails regarding new orders, system shipments, direct
shipments, order updates, and tracking notifications

Resin Mixing & Material Preparation
● Accurately measure, weigh, and mix resin formulations in accordance with work
instructions and safety protocols
● Prepare standard and custom resin blends to support internal production and customer
orders
● Ensure proper labeling, traceability, and documentation of mixed materials
● Maintain clean mixing equipment, tools, and work areas in accordance with safety and
quality standards
● Follow all hazardous material handling, storage, and disposal guidelines
General
● Maintain a clean, organized, and safe work environment
● Perform additional duties as assigned by management
Basic Qualifications
● High School Diploma or equivalent
● Strong PC skills, including proficiency with Microsoft Office Suite
● Familiarity with ERP/MRP systems; experience with FedEx and UPS shipping software
is a plus
● Ability to obtain internal forklift operator certification
● HAZMAT shipping experience is a plus
● Experience working with chemicals, resins, or material mixing processes is preferred
● Experience using basic tools common in warehouse/logistics operations
● Ability to work effectively in a dynamic, team-oriented environment

● Strong written and verbal communication skills
● Excellent attention to detail with the ability to multitask
● Valid Driver’s License
Physical Requirements
● Spend the majority of the workday on foot
● Ability to lift packages and materials up to 55 lbs
● Ability to wear required Personal Protective Equipment (PPE), including safety glasses,
gloves, and other protective gear as required for chemical handling

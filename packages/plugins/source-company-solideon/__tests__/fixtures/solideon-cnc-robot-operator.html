2025 CNC & ROBOT OPERATOR

Salary Recommendation: $80-100k 

Location: Berkeley, California (On-Site. Relocation expenses not provided.)

Company Overview: 

Solideon is a leader in advanced manufacturing, leveraging robotics and proprietary technology to build autonomous micro-factories capable of producing, assembling, and inspecting entire structures without human intervention. We aim to push the boundaries of manufacturing to shape a future where technology and humanity intersect.

Position Overview: 

The CNC & Robot Technician will play a key role in supporting the ongoing R&D and production efforts. You’ll report to the head of engineering and will work in the machine shop to develop requisite one-off parts to assist across projects, leveraging heavy equipment like industrial robotic arms and CNC machines. This role is ideal for someone experienced in automated systems, manufacturing, and metrology with a passion for innovation.

Responsibilities:

  • Program and operate CNC machines to perform tasks such as routers, grinding, milling, etc.
  • Translate instructions into computer commands so the machines can perform the correct function.
  • Check and maintain machinery daily to ensure functionality. Maintain quality control documents and update setup information
  • Operate and monitor robotic systems remotely to inspect and maintain manufacturing cells
  • Collaborate with the technical team to troubleshoot and resolve any issues related to robot operation
  • Ensure that robotic systems are operating within specified parameters and safety standards
  • Operate, maintain, and troubleshoot robotics and manufacturing equipment.
  • Fabricate, assemble, test, inspect, and maintain production tools and systems.
  • Machine parts using detailed drawings and CAD models
  • Assist with the development of robotics-enabled machining functions

Qualifications:

  • Extensive knowledge and experience reading and designing CAD and CAM systems
  • Proficiency in reading engineering prints, schematics, and assembly instructions.
  • Ability to lift up to 50 lbs and stand for extended periods.
  • Demonstrated experience working with CNC machines
  • Must be a US Citizen

Preferred Skills:

  • Previous experience working with robotic arms made by manufacturers like Kuka, Kawasaki, Fanuc, Yaskawa, ABB, etc.
  • Welding, Metal Fabrication, or Construction
  • Experience writing PLC code and programming CNC machines
  • Familiarity with ISO9001 or other quality manufacturing processes

Why Solideon?

  • Join a dynamic, forward-thinking team at the cutting edge of advanced manufacturing.
  • Contribute to innovative projects that aim to reshape the future of production.

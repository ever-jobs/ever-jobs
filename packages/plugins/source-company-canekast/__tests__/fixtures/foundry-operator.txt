• Minimum of a high school diploma or equivalent

Foundry Operator – Role Responsibilities
Job duties may include some/all of the following: melting and pouring molten aluminum; making sand
molds; removing castings from molds; making sand cores; using power and hand tools for sawing,
grinding, and finishing castings. We will cross-train all positions to provide you with the skills necessary
for a career in the foundry trade .
• Strong attention to detail
• Jobs are physically demanding with repetitive operations, must be to stand throughout the
day, lift 30-50lbs frequently and be able to bend, twist, grasp and reach throughout the day
• Inspects each mold to ensure quality
• Performs pattern inspection prior to starting job
• Operate manual molding machines to make sand molds
• Operate automatic molding machines to make sand molds
• Able to perform changeover of patterns for next jobs to be run
• All other duties as assigned by management

About the role

Operate and maintain tube laser cutting equipment to produce precision-cut tubing and structural components. Set up programs, load material, monitor cut quality, and perform basic machine maintenance.

Requirements

  • Experience operating CNC or laser cutting equipment
  • Ability to read and interpret engineering drawings and cut sheets
  • Basic understanding of material types (steel, stainless, aluminum tubing)
  • Strong attention to detail and commitment to quality
  • High school diploma or equivalent

Preferred qualifications

  • Prior tube laser or pipe cutting experience
  • Familiarity with nesting software
  • Forklift or overhead crane experience

Benefits

  • Competitive pay based on experience
  • Health, dental, and vision insurance
  • 401(k) with company match
  • Paid time off and holidays
  • Overtime opportunities
  • On-the-job training

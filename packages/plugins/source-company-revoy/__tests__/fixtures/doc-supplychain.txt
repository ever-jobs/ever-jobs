Revoy — Lead Supply Chain Engineer


Location: Troutdale, OR (on-site)
Reports to: CTO
Department: Operations / Engineering
Type: Full-time
About the Role
Revoy is building a battery-electric converter dolly that instantly converts Class 8 tractor-trailer combinations into 96% electric power. We're moving from prototype to commercialization, and we're hiring our first dedicated Supply Chain Engineer to own the function end-to-end, from sourcing strategy and supplier development into series production.
You'll inherit an existing BOM and active supplier pipeline (>100 suppliers already engaged across axles, brakes, suspension, thermal, HV components, connectors, and controls) and turn it into a disciplined, auditable, production-ready supply base. You'll work shoulder-to-shoulder with our mechanical, electrical, and firmware engineers, and you'll be directly responsible for BOM cost, manufacturability, and timeline.
Some travel will be required to interface face to face with suppliers and contract manufacturers
What You'll Own
* Sourcing strategy for the full vehicle BOM. This includes everything: heavy-duty mechanical components (axles, suspension, air brakes, kingpin/fifth wheel, frame), HV electrical (battery pack/modules, inverters, motors, OBC, DC-DC, contactors, HV harness), LV electronics, thermal management (chillers, coolant loops, plates), and structural fabrication
* Supplier qualification and development. You’ll be expected to produce RFQ packages, technical reviews, capacity assessments, on-site audits, PPAP/PSW execution
* Should-cost models and negotiation for both legacy truck Tier 1s (Dana, Meritor/Cummins, Bendix/Knorr, Hendrickson, SAF-Holland, etc.) and EV-native suppliers (cell, pack, power electronics, traction motor)
* Supply Chain Risk management, for example single vs. dual-source decisions, geographic risk, and tariff treatment sourcing implications, long lead-time forecasting
* NPI execution, including  DFM/DFA loops with engineering, prototype-to-pilot-to-production transitions, ECO management with suppliers
* Logistics and inventory for prototype builds today, scaling to pilot production fleet deployments
* The cost roadmap from EVT through SOP and into volume
Required Qualifications
* 5+ years in supply chain, sourcing, or supplier quality engineering for complex hardware such as commercial vehicles, EVs, aerospace, heavy equipment, or industrial machinery
* Engineering degree (Mechanical, Electrical, Manufacturing, Industrial) or equivalent hands-on experience. You can read a drawing, interpret a GD&T callout, understand a schematic, and push back on a supplier's technical claim
* Direct experience taking a hardware product through NPI. APQP, PPAP, FMEA, control plans, first-article inspection
* Demonstrated negotiation track record with real dollar outcomes you can speak to
* EV powertrain sourcing for battery cells/modules/packs, traction inverters, motors, BMS, HV interconnect. We’d love for you to have existing relationships to hit the ground running
Strongly Preferred
* Class 8 / heavy commercial vehicle experience. You know the Tier 1 landscape, you understand FMVSS implications for trailers vs. tractors, you've worked with J1939, air brake systems, or heavy-duty drivetrain components
* Experience with IATF 16949 or AS9100 quality systems, and an opinion on which parts of them are worth keeping at this stage of company maturity
* Hands-on time on a shop floor or in a supplier facility
* Track record taking a vehicle program from prototype through DV / PV and into production

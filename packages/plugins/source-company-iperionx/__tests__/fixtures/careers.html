Careers

Careers at IperionX

Thank you for your interest in becoming part of the world-class team at IperionX.

We are currently scaling production at our Virginia Titanium Manufacturing Campus, comprising the Titanium Production Facility and the Advanced Manufacturing Center at the Southern Virginia Technology Park in Halifax County, Virginia.

We encourage you to explore our website and learn more about who we are and what we do. Stay connected with us through our newsletter and social media channels for the latest opportunities and company updates.

Current Openings

Manufacturing Engineer - Virginia

IperionX is seeking a skilled Manufacturing Engineer to join our team at the Titanium Manufacturing Campus in the Southern Virginia Technology Park. In this role, you will play a key part in designing, developing, and optimizing manufacturing processes to improve production efficiency, enhance product quality, and ensure all operations meet rigorous safety and quality standards.

Apply Now

Quality Coordinator - Virginia

IperionX is seeking a Quality Coordinator to join our team at IperionX’s Titanium Manufacturing Campus in the Southern Virginia Technology Park. In this role, you will be responsible for overseeing and maintaining the company’s Quality Management System (QMS), ensuring compliance with applicable ISO standards, and supporting ongoing certification readiness. You will play a key role in driving continuous improvement initiatives, coordinating internal audits, and managing documentation control processes to uphold quality and operational excellence. This position reports directly to the Senior Quality Engineer and serves as a critical contributor to IperionX’s commitment to high-performance manufacturing and quality assurance.

Apply Now

Production Supervisor - Night - Virginia

IperionX is seeking a Production Supervisor (Night) to join our team at the Titanium Manufacturing Campus in the Southern Virginia Technology Park. In this role, you will support the scale-up of our innovative, low-carbon titanium manufacturing operations by leading safe, efficient chemical and metallurgical production processes. As a frontline leader, you will oversee hourly operators, ensure production and quality targets are met, and maintain a strong presence on the floor while collaborating closely with Engineering, Maintenance, Quality, and EHS teams to drive reliable operations and continuous improvement.

Apply Now

Advanced Manufacturing Engineer - Virginia

IperionX is seeking an Advanced Manufacturing Engineer to join our team at IperionX’s Titanium Manufacturing Campus in the Southern Virginia Technology Park. In this role, you will be responsible for driving the development and production of complex metal components using cutting-edge additive manufacturing technologies, with a strong focus on Binder Jetting and Laser Powder Bed Fusion (LPBF). You’ll play a critical part in advancing our manufacturing capabilities from print preparation and process optimization to post-processing and full production readiness ensuring high-quality, scalable output. As a key contributor, you will collaborate cross-functionally with engineering, operations, quality, and customer teams to bridge the gap between innovation and execution, helping bring next-generation titanium products from concept to reality.

Apply Now

R&D Lab Technician - Virginia

IperionX is seeking an R&D Lab Technician to join our team at IperionX’s Titanium Manufacturing Campus in the Southern Virginia Technology Park. In this role, you will be responsible for assisting in the execution of complex, cross-functional projects focused on scale-up, commercialization, and optimization of particulate processing technologies for minerals and metals. This role operates in a fast-paced, high-accountability environment and ensures that technical, safety, schedule, and cost objectives are consistently met. This is an entry-level role that reports to the R&D Operations Manager, Process Development.

Apply Now

Maintenance Manager - Virginia

IperionX is seeking a Maintenance Manager to join our team at IperionX’s Titanium Manufacturing Campus in the Southern Virginia Technology Park. In this role, you will be responsible for leading a skilled team of maintenance professionals to support 24/7 operations, driving continuous improvement initiatives to reduce equipment downtime, and owning a proactive maintenance program aligned with Lean Total Productive Maintenance principles. You will oversee budgeting, safety, and compliance programs, while leading root cause analysis, managing capital projects, and collaborating cross-functionally to optimize equipment performance, reliability, and throughput across the facility.

Apply Now
